dmarcator

Generate DNS records that lock down a parked (non-sending) domain: null MX, SPF -all, and DMARC p=reject.

Only use this on domains that send no email. Publishing these records on a domain that sends mail will make that mail fail. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
